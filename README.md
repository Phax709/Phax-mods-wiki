# Phax709 Studios — Minecraft Mods Wiki

Official website and documentation hub for Minecraft mods created by Phax709.

**Website:** [phax709.github.io/Phax-mods-wiki](https://phax709.github.io/Phax-mods-wiki/)

## Mods

- [Acatar](https://phax709.github.io/Phax-mods-wiki/pages/mods/acatar.html) — an adventure mod with its own creatures, resources, biomes, and dimension.
  - [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/acatar) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/acatar)
- [Chaosium](https://phax709.github.io/Phax-mods-wiki/pages/mods/chaosium.html) — a magic- and chaos-themed Minecraft mod.
  - [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/chaosium) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/chaosium)
- [Phax Sirens](https://phax709.github.io/Phax-mods-wiki/pages/mods/phax-sirens.html) — a Minecraft mod focused on sirens and alarms.
  - [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/phax-sirens) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/phax-sirens)
- [Phax: Potion Time+](https://phax709.github.io/Phax-mods-wiki/pages/mods/potion-time-plus.html) — adds extended-duration potion options.
  - [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/phax-potion-time) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/phax-potion-time+)

## What the website includes

- Wiki pages for mod items, blocks, creatures, resources, recipes, and other content.
- Mod development statuses and project information.
- News and release patch notes.
- Guides for installing mods and finding support.

The language selector offers nine locales. Detailed mod and wiki content is currently available primarily in English and French; other locales may use English fallback text.
